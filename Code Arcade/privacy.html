Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what Code Arcade (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website and how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you choose to submit through our contact, quote or client-portal forms: name, email address, selected service, budget range, project description and timeline preferences.
  • Technical data your browser sends to any website (IP address, browser type, requested pages) which our hosting provider may log for security and reliability.

We do not use advertising trackers, and this site does not set marketing cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare quotes and deliver projects you engage us for.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to us by a third-party form-delivery service or by your own email app. Our pages load fonts and icons from Google Fonts and cdnjs, which means your IP address is visible to those providers when a page loads. The site is hosted on Vercel.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for the life of the project and a reasonable period afterwards for support and accounting. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing codearcade20@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
